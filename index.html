About BuildCV

        

          BuildCV is a simple and practical online platform designed to help developers and job seekers
          create professional, ATS-friendly resumes quickly. It combines CV creation, live preview,
          JSON-based resume generation, interview preparation resources, and developer-focused learning
          tools in one place.
        

        

          The official website is
          https://www.buildcv.co.in/.
